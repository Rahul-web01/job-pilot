Outreach

  1. Open a job, scroll to Email HR or the hiring manager and press Find emails.
  2. Press Write email next to a contact. The local AI drafts it with your resume attached.
  3. Edit it here or on the job page, then press Send. It goes from your Gmail.
  4. No reply after 6 days? A follow-up shows up below. Send one follow-up at most.

To send, add your Gmail and an App Password on the Profile tab.
